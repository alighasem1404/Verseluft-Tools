2. Text and spreadsheet cells

Wrap each exact demo value in $, then add $$$ and its cell address. One content block can include multiple paragraphs.

Example: $Black Bell Company$$$D2$
Line breaks inside the content are preserved exactly. Sheet-qualified addresses such as 'Character Data'!D2 and absolute addresses such as $D$2 are supported. Markdown marks such as ##, **, and {{...}} do not stop an exact match.
